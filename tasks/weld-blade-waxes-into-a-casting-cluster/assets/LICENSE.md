# Assets

Every mesh in this folder is generated by `make_assets.py` from the numbers in `cluster.py` and `task.py`; none comes from a third party. Released with the task under the repository's licence.

- `blade_pattern_master.stl`: the blade pattern as one solid (gate, fir-tree root, platform, airfoil), in millimetres. Print it (0.1 mm layers), stand it gate face down in `mould_box.stl`, fill the box with RTV silicone, cut the cured block open along the airfoil's chord and pour patterns in blue injection wax.
- `mould_box.stl`: an open box with 3 mm walls, 10 mm clear of the master all round.
- `knife_rest.stl`: the knife's two cradles with side rails and the blade guard, in the rest's own frame (blade centre at the origin).
